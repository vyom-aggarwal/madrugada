      About Madrugada

      Same hours. Smarter dawn.

      An advisory day briefing for field crews: forecast temperature and air quality for a replayed day, a work plan that starts no earlier than first light, and the hours scheduling can't fix. Numbers are modeled exposure-hours and forecast-based estimates.

      It does not replace the employer's written Cal/OSHA Heat Illness Prevention Plan. Water, shade and rest requirements still apply.
